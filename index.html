About

Luigi Giovanetti.
FX Artist.

An Italian FX artist drawn to the craft of bringing effects to life. Great FX don't carry a film — but they can lift a great one to another level, and quietly rescue a struggling one. That challenge is exactly what I love about this work. My hope is that what I do inspires others to pursue the creative path they love, just as others inspired me to pursue mine.
